03 / CONCEPT

Topoi Topiary

Concepts grow in relation.

A topos is a cultivated problem space. Begin with a question, return to a text, register disagreement, and test what the concept does in practice. Sixteen worked entries connect the early question of care with later questions of technics, art, and event. Performance opens an explicitly marked path for thinking with Heidegger.

T1 / EARLY CROSSING

Care

How are we already involved in a world and open to possibilities?

Text. Being and Time articulates Dasein’s being as care (Sorge), distinguished from concern (Besorgen) and solicitude (Fürsorge). Disagreement. Does an account centered on Dasein sufficiently describe dependency and shared forms of care?

Danger. The call to own one’s life can be read as heroic self-sufficiency. Counter-practice. Name the people, infrastructures, and histories making a situation possible.

StudioLab form. In D0, list three claims the situation makes on you before proposing a design.

Cross to Gestell: whose care becomes availability on a schedule?

T2 / CROSS-TURN

World

How does a background of significance let anything show up?

Text. Worldhood in Being and Time begins from involvement, not a container of objects. Disagreement. What changes when plural ways of worlding are described by their participants, rather than inferred through one philosophical vocabulary?

Danger. “A world” can become a sealed cultural unit. Counter-practice. Follow exchanges, translations, technical mediations, and internal disagreements.

StudioLab form. Apply two Atlas grammars to one encounter; invite a correction from someone within it.

Cross to Gestell: when does orderability become a background of significance?

T3 / LATE CROSSING

Thing / Fourfold

What does a thing gather, and who is left outside its gathering?

Text. Building Dwelling Thinking asks how building belongs to dwelling. Disagreement. Can the fourfold disclose a place while still accounting for its labor, property, and conflict?

Danger. Poetic quietism can beautify a place while ignoring its conditions. Counter-practice. Read the object’s materials, maintenance, access, and contested uses.

StudioLab form. Bring one ordinary thing to the Sixfold studio and test its gathering across different worlds.

Cross to standing-reserve: what changes when a thing appears chiefly as on-call stock?

T4 / ARCHIVE CROSSING

Gestell / Standing-Reserve

How does what exists come to appear as orderable and on call?

Text. GA 7 distinguishes the ordering mode (Ge-stell) from what appears as available (Bestand); GA 79 reopens the question of positionality and replaceability. Disagreement. What changes across the Bremen lecture, the later essay, and their English translations?

Danger. A sweeping diagnosis may erase differences in place, labor, and power. Counter-practice. Keep a primary passage, a reader’s interpretation, and an objection on the same page.

BeyngLab form. A four-stop Ereignis trail with Babich, Mitchell, Kisiel, and Wise as distinct interlocutors.

Read the full T4 Topos ↗Read Heidegger at Ereignis ↗
T13 / LETTING AND THINKING

Release / Gelassenheit

How can thinking let go of mastery without abandoning responsibility?

Text. Read the Memorial Address alongside Country Path Conversations, with their different dates, versions, and translations. Disagreement. What can a freer relation to technology change, and what material conditions does it leave unresolved?

Danger. Release becomes resignation or another productivity technique. Counter-practice. Keep a concrete obligation and another person’s objection beside the proposal to let go.

Readers. Davis, Dreyfus, and Glazebrook, with a further route through Schürmann.

Read the full Release Topos
T16 / REFLECTION AND QUESTIONING

Mindfulness / Besinnung

What changes when thinking questions its own assumptions?

Text. Follow meditation in GA 65, Besinnung in GA 66, and reflection on science in GA 7. Disagreement. What lets reflective thinking recognize its own blindness?

Danger. A critique of other methods exempts itself from correction. Counter-practice. Put one working measure and your own interpretation under question.

Readers. Babich, Berger, and Vallega-Neu at Ereignis.

Read the full Mindfulness Topos
T14 / ARCHIVE CROSSING

Nihilism

Does making new values overcome nihilism—or continue it?

Read devaluation and forgottenness through GA 5, with Pippin, Mitchell, and Fried. Keep the diagnosis situated and its political stakes open to criticism.

Read the full Nihilism Topos
T15 / ARCHIVE CROSSING

Nothingness / das Nichts

How can the nothing disclose beings without becoming another being?

Read anxiety, worldhood, and What Is Metaphysics? with Withy, Wise, and Carman. Keep the nothing distinct from nihilism and clinical anxiety.

Read the full Nothingness / das Nichts Topos
T5 / ARCHIVE CROSSING

Call / Ruf

How can a call come from me without being something I choose to say?

Begin with the call of conscience in Being and Time. Follow its unsettling relation to selfhood and care, then ask what kind of response it makes possible. Keep this analysis distinct from later accounts of the call of being.

Read the full Call / Ruf Topos
T6 / ARCHIVE CROSSING

Authenticity / Eigentlichkeit

How can existence become one’s own while remaining thrown into a shared world?

Read authenticity through possibility, finitude, and the retrieval of a past. This path asks how a way of existing changes; it offers no personality test and no ranking of people or cultures.

Read the full Authenticity / Eigentlichkeit Topos
T8 / ARCHIVE CROSSING

Dwelling / Wohnen

What does a place let happen—and whose dwelling does it sustain?

Follow Building Dwelling Thinking from sparing to gathering and lived space. Keep the poetic account in conversation with questions of access, maintenance, and displacement. Those questions belong to our testing of the text, not to a ready-made housing policy in Heidegger.

Read the full Dwelling / Wohnen Topos
T9 / ARCHIVE CROSSING

Event / Ereignis

How can we think what lets being and time belong together without turning it into another thing?

Read Ereignis across Contributions to Philosophy and Time and Being. Preserve the difference between the 1930s manuscripts and the 1962 lecture, and keep translation choices visible. “Event” is an entry into a problem, not a synonym for any dramatic occurrence.

Read the full Event / Ereignis Topos
T7 / ARCHIVE CROSSING

Difference

Must difference begin with identities—or can identity emerge through difference?

Begin with Identity and Difference: Heidegger’s thinking of belonging-together and his encounter with Hegel. Follow distinct crossings into Derrida’s différance and Deleuze’s difference in itself, while keeping the earlier ontological question and other sources of twentieth-century difference in view.

Read the full Difference Topos
T12 / ARCHIVE CROSSING

Technē

What changes when technē is read as a way of revealing rather than simply a means of making?

Follow Heidegger’s interpretation of technē through knowing, bringing-forth, and the question of modern technology. Read the Greek inheritance as an argument with a history, not as an uncontested dictionary definition.

Read the full Technē Topos
T11 / ARCHIVE CROSSING

Poiesis

How does bringing something forth change what can appear?

Read bringing-forth alongside the work of art’s opening of truth. Keep poiēsis, poetry, manufacture, and creative novelty distinct. A new product does not automatically disclose a new world.

Read the full Poiesis Topos
T10 / THINKING WITH

Performance

How does doing become a demand to perform—and who decides what counts?

Read practical involvement with Heidegger, labor and the performance principle with Marcuse, and performativity with Lyotard. The shared question is how ways of doing become demands that organize existence. Their concepts differ; this Topos makes the connections available for testing.

Read the full Performance Topos
