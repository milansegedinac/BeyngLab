Three Heideggers Helyx.

Care, the history of Being, and dwelling recur as interwoven questions. The plate proposes crossings among them rather than a line of intellectual progress.

Read the plate

The argument in words.

  1. Care / 1927–33. Begin with Dasein already involved in a world. The question is not the isolated subject’s control but its situated concern. The danger is heroic authenticity.
  2. History of Being / 1933–45. Ask how a historical clearing orders what can appear. The danger is mythopolitical capture; Heidegger’s political entanglement belongs to the reading.
  3. Thing and dwelling / 1945–76. Attend to gathering and place. The danger is poetic quietism that omits labor and conflict.
  4. Crossings. Read “world” through all three. A later coil reopens rather than resolves the earlier question. Use the Turns and Topoi to follow a crossing.
LEGEND

Large circles = recurrent reading orientations. Curved paths = conceptual crossings. Rust labels = capture risks, carried into any application.
