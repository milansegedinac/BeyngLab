Regional Daseins Atlas.

A pause precedes the categories. Six grammars are shown as overlapping ways of reading an encounter, each with a risk. No card stands for a nation, ethnicity, or sealed tradition.

Read the plate

The argument in words.

  1. D0. Pause and ask what already acts in the situation. This interval is optional and unscored.
  2. Attunement → worlding → dwelling. Notice felt orientation, trace how significance is made, then ask how a way of living holds or fails.
  3. Six lenses. Imaginal asks how an image appears and mediates a world; Metanoetic reads a turn after failure; Pluriversal reads relational territory; Mythic-Traditionalist diagnoses mythic hunger; Pharmacological reads technics and attention; Cosmotechnical reads plural technical worlds.
  4. Paired capture. Respectively: dehistoricization, guilt capture, romantic localism, mythopolitical hierarchy, therapeutic technocracy, comparative essentialism. Select multiple lenses; let partners revise or reject them.
LEGEND

Blue path = a proposed method. Six cards = overlapping operations. Rust line = danger, printed on the same card. Connections do not claim descent or a fixed geography.
