Visual argument / version 1.0

After Corbin: branches, not a lineage.

This plate traces a question through Corbin’s imaginal and into different afterlives. The film and art row is a set of BeyngLab reading resonances, not proof of influence.

Read the plate

The argument in words.

  1. Question and hinge. Heideggerian worldhood and hermeneutics meet Corbin’s turn to Suhrawardi, ta’wil, and the imaginal. The relation is a historical reception to research, not a merger of traditions.
  2. Disputed afterlives. Fardid raises mythopolitical danger; Shayegan’s self-critique complicates dualism; Nasr is an adjacent traditionalist line; El-Bizri invites comparison with Avicenna and phenomenology. These are distinct routes.
  3. Film and art. Kiarostami, Bayzai, Farmanfarmaian, and Neshat are proposed close-reading encounters. The plate marks them Experimental because direct influence is not asserted.
  4. Return. The Ta’wil Portal asks what an image discloses and what this reading obscures. Attach a risk and an objection before making a design claim.
LEGEND

Solid blue = historical hinge to investigate. Thin blue = editorial comparison. Dashed blue = BeyngLab resonance. Rust return = danger and revision. The arrows organize questions; they do not prove influence.
