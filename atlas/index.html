01 / FIELD

Atlas of Regional Daseins

Ways a world takes shape.

A region here is an operation of disclosure. It can travel across places, coexist with other operations, and change when people challenge our description. These six cards are working lenses, not identities to assign to cultures.

READING RULE / 01

Read across each card: an operation, what it lets us notice, and how that operation can be captured. Use more than one card for a situation; ask the people involved where this atlas misses them.

Six working grammars

The field, with its risks.

Imaginal

Operation: Corbin names a mode of perception and an order of reality; BeyngLab asks how an image mediates disclosure in a particular encounter.

PossibilityRead the depth and mediation of an image.
DangerAngelic abstraction can erase history and material conditions.

Metanoetic

D0 / BEFORE INTERVENTION

The interval before the design.

Stop long enough to notice what already matters. “Non-thought” and “non-action” are working names for refusing an automatic solution; they do not forbid thinking or action. “Perfumance” and “infinihil” are the report’s experimental language, held here as prompts rather than settled concepts.

  1. Pause
  2. Attune
  3. World
  4. Dwell

D0 is a method across the site, not a seventh region, therapeutic score, or required gateway.

Provenance

Experimental These cards synthesize the AIR V report into editable design lenses. The named thinkers orient research; this short atlas does not stand in for their texts. For Heidegger and technology, read The Question Concerning Technology at Ereignis.
