02 / TIME

Turns Labyrinth

Three turns, no final stage.

These are reading orientations, not a ladder of improvement. Questions about care, history, and dwelling return in altered forms; a later text does not simply erase an earlier one.

011927–33

Early / Dasein and care

How is a life already involved in a world?

In Being and Time, Dasein is being-in-the-world rather than an isolated observer. Care, thrownness, and being-with let us ask how significance is already there before a detached description.

CareWorldhoodBeing-with
Watch for

A heroic picture of authenticity that forgets dependence and shared worlds.

021933–45

Middle / history of Being

What if an epoch shapes what can appear?

The question turns toward the history of Being, the event of appropriation (Ereignis), and ways an age orders beings. This turn must be read alongside Heidegger’s political entanglement, not insulated from it.

EreignisHistoryMachination
Watch for

Mythopolitical capture: an epochal story can authorize hierarchy and exclusion.

031945–76

Late / thing and fourfold

How might a thing gather a place?

Dwelling, the fourfold, and the thing shift attention toward gathering and letting-be. A built place can be read for how it draws together earth, sky, mortals, and divinities without treating these terms as a design recipe.

ThingFourfoldDwelling
Watch for

Poetic quietism that turns dwelling into withdrawal from material conflict.
