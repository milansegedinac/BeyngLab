W1 / PRACTICE

Workshops / prototype bench

Try a reading. Keep the objection.

Two exercises are ready for a small group or a solo session. Both move from description to interpretation and ask whose voice the reading omits. The remaining prototypes are sketches.

PROTOTYPE 01 / RUNNABLE

Ta’wil Portal

30 minutes · 1–6 people

Bring an image, text, object, or place. Use paper or the worksheet below. A facilitator may read the prompts aloud.

Scope

This exercise borrows a question from the Imaginal grammar. It does not confer authority in a living religious practice or reproduce ta’wil as practiced in its own traditions.

  1. 03′ / D0

    Pause at the threshold.

    Look without naming a solution. What has already arranged this encounter? Anyone may skip the pause.

  2. 05′ / FORM

    Describe what is here.

    Record visible or audible features without immediately explaining what they mean. Include material, setting, maker, and access where known.

  3. 07′ / WORLD

    Follow what it opens.

    What relations, histories, or ways of attending become possible through it? Offer more than one reading if the group differs.

  4. 07′ / WITHHELD

    Ask what is absent.

    Whose interpretation is missing? What labor, institution, or conflict would a beautiful reading make hard to see?

  5. 08′ / RETURN

    Revise the claim.

    State a provisional insight, a danger, and a counter-practice. Invite a partner’s correction; keep disagreement in the record.
