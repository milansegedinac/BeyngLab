P1 / PATHWAY

A guided reception field / five stations

Sufi Heidegger after Corbin.

“Sufi Heidegger” is BeyngLab’s working name for a contested route through texts and images. It is not the name of an established school, an identity for Islamicate philosophy, or a claim that every artist here read Heidegger.

01HEIDEGGERIAN QUESTION

What lets a world appear?

Begin from worldhood and hermeneutics: understanding is always situated. The question of nothing, language, and mortality gives later readers several entrances; no single Heideggerian answer governs what follows.

PrimaryBeing and Time (GA 2) ↗Trace the Turns ↗

Test the danger. A supposedly universal question may still carry the limits of its origin and vocabulary.

02CORBIN AS HINGE

What can an image disclose?

Henry Corbin’s route from Heideggerian hermeneutics toward Suhrawardi, ta’wil, and the mundus imaginalis makes the image more than an illustration of a prior concept. Here, the imaginal proposes a mode of world disclosure. The passage is a historical and interpretive hinge, not a claim that Heidegger’s categories contain these traditions.

Historical receptionRead the Imaginal grammar ↗

Test the danger. Spiritual geography can become angelic abstraction, losing institutions, bodies, and history.

04FILM & ART

Let the works interrupt the map.

The following are BeyngLab resonances: proposals for close reading, not assertions of direct Heideggerian influence or religious identity. A future documented reception claim needs its own evidence.

KiarostamiThe Wind Will Carry Us: offscreen lives, delay, and the visiting camera’s power. Compare two lenses ↗
BayzaiTravelers and ritual threshold. Attend to the film’s own form and context.
FarmanfarmaianMirror geometry and divided perspective. Let material, craft, and bodily position matter.
NeshatVeil, calligraphy, voice, and Logic of the Birds. Avoid collapsing distinct works into one symbol.

Test the danger. Never call Kiarostami a “Sufi filmmaker” on the strength of this juxtaposition. Do not turn an artist’s work into a philosophical illustration.

05RETURN TO PRACTICE

Read, then revise.

Choose one image, place, or object. Describe what it offers to sight; what world it opens; and what the reading leaves out. Name a danger before turning an interpretation into a design claim. If a participant contests a category, keep the objection with the diagram.

How to read the evidence

Historical A documented reception must be checked in a named source. Interpretive A scholarly comparison reads works together without claiming direct influence. Experimental A BeyngLab resonance is a proposed encounter to test with the works. This pathway follows the AIR V report; further bibliography is an editorial task before expanding the individual branches.
